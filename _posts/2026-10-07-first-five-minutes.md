Most tickets are won or lost before anyone opens a deep troubleshooting rabbit hole. The first five minutes should gather facts, not prove cleverness.

## Ask for the same four things

1. **Exact error text** or a screenshot with the timestamp visible
2. **Device and account** in use when it failed
3. **What changed recently**: app update, password reset, new hardware, travel, VPN
4. **Who else is affected**: only this user, a team, or a location

## Do the quick checks first

- Confirm the user is signed into the right account
- Check whether the service is up for others
- Reproduce once if it is safe and cheap to reproduce
- Look for a matching ticket from the last week

## Write while you work

Leave a short note before you escalate or step away. Future-you, and the next tech, should be able to pick up without a hallway briefing.

A disciplined first five minutes cuts reopen rates. It also makes the hard tickets obvious faster, because the easy explanations are already ruled out.
