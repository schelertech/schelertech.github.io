## A simple restart checklist for any helpdesk

If you are also dusting off process after a busy stretch, start here:

1. **Define intake.** One place for requests. Email, chat, and hallway asks all need a ticket.
2. **Name the priority rules.** Urgent should mean business impact, not whoever shouts first.
3. **Standardize the first five minutes.** Verify identity, device, recent changes, and error text before deep diving.
4. **Write the fix down.** If it took more than ten minutes once, it will happen again.
5. **Review repeats weekly.** The same three issues usually create half the noise.
